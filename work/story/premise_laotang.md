# 《老汤》— pilot premise, ep01–ep03

**Status:** draft for approval. Not a shot list yet — that comes after you sign off the beats.
**Format:** 红果 vertical, 9:16, 90 s per episode, 60-episode season target.
**Look:** one hero interior set (火锅店), one street strip, one back-alley doorway. No location moves.
**Territory:** every shot `both` — no engine with a territorial exclusion is used anywhere in this story.

---

## Logline

被二叔当众夺走老店和母亲留下的老汤配方那天，苏念只捡走了一口被扔在后巷的旧铜锅。
三年后，同一条街上，那口锅前排的队，比老店长。

**Alt title (红果 A/B):** 《被赶出家门那天，我捡走了那口锅》

---

## Why this premise, production-wise

| Requirement | How the story satisfies it |
|---|---|
| One set, used 90 % of runtime | Nearly everything happens at one table in one room |
| Small cast, face-driven | 4 leads, max 3 in frame at once |
| Cheap VFX | Steam, rain outside the window, warm practicals — all generated by the video model |
| Reversal every ~30 s | The 掀锅 beat, the 捡锅 beat, the 顾衍 line |
| No crowd, no extras | The "queue" is heard, not shown, until episode 40 |
| CG-friendly | Clay/depth guide passes carry structure; the video model supplies skin and steam |

## Episodes 2–3 (summary, for set planning)

- **ep02 — 排队:** The stall's third night. 苏建国 sends a 市监 inspection; 顾衍 handles it with one phone call, which 苏念 notices. Reversal: she refuses his help.
- **ep03 — 老周:** 老周 tastes the broth, recognises the pot as his own master's, and tells her the recipe was stolen, not sold. First 打脸 of 苏建国, in his own shop, at his own table.

Everything in ep02–03 uses the same three set pieces. No new builds.
